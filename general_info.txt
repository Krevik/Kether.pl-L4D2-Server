Prop Hunt (matchmode "prophunt", plugin kether_prophunt.smx):
-classic hide-and-seek gamemode built on top of mp_gamemode "versus", plugin-managed round loop
	on a continuous rotation (many short rounds per map instead of the native 2-round versus flow)
-two teams: Hunters (survivors, the seekers) and Props (infected, the hiders)
-Hunters are picked each round via a fairness counter + volunteer queue (!hunt to volunteer);
	everyone else plays as a Prop
-Props spawn as weaponless, attack-disabled Hunters (m_zombieClass forced to Hunter) - they
	cannot pounce, claw or otherwise fight back
-round starts with a hide phase: Hunters are frozen and blinded while Props pick a disguise
	by aiming at a world prop (entity or static) and pressing +use; a menu (!prop) is the
	fallback/admin path
-disguised Props can rotate freely, use !lock to freeze themselves in place (blocked over an
	active hazard trigger), get an automatic thirdperson camera, and taunt (!taunt) with a
	special infected sound on a cooldown
-once the seek phase starts, Props periodically and automatically emit a random special
	infected sound (every ~45s by default, with a warning beforehand) so they can't camp
	silently forever
-Hunters lose HP for every shot fired and regain HP for landing hits, with a bonus for
	eliminating a Prop - encourages careful, confirmed shots instead of spraying every prop
	in sight
-round ends when every Prop has been found (Hunters win) or the round timer expires with at
	least one Prop still alive (Props win)
-no common infected, no AI special infected, no tank/witch; weapon/item spawns and any way to
	end the map (changelevel/finale/rescue triggers) are stripped out via cfg/stripper/prophunt
-anti-cheat scan kicks Hunters caught with r_staticpropinfo enabled, since that cvar reveals
	static prop names/bounds and would trivially expose disguised Props
-tunable via ph_* cvars (see plugin_commands_cvars.md), commands: !hunt, !prop, !lock, !taunt, !ph
